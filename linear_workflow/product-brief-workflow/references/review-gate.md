# Product Brief Review Gate

Review for:
1. Target user and context are specific enough to inform MVP planning.
2. Problem is separated from solution; its supporting evidence is labeled.
3. Value proposition and goals align with the stated problem.
4. Metrics are measurable candidates; unknown baselines and targets are explicitly TBD.
5. Constraints, non-goals, risks, assumptions and open questions are visible.
6. Key decisions have user confirmation and a traceable rationale.
7. No unsupported market claims, fabricated interviews, or invented requirements.
8. No premature PRD, UX/UI specifications, or development commitments.

Produce a review table with `criterion | pass/needs work | evidence | action`. Ask the user to choose `Approve`, `Revise`, or `Approve with conditions` (conditions explicitly recorded). Only explicit user approval permits Approved status. Do not silently treat approval of an individual stage as final approval.
