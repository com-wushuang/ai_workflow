# Product Brief Template

Canonical path: `docs/product/Product-Brief.md`

```markdown
---
title: <Product name> — Product Brief
status: Draft
version: 0.1
linear_project: <verified project name or identifier>
last_reviewed: <date if known>
---

# Product Brief — <Product name>

## 1. Product Overview
One-sentence concept and context.

## 2. Target Users and Context
Primary user, situations, and evidence/uncertainty.

## 3. Problem Statement
Problem (not proposed solution), frequency/impact, evidence and gaps.

## 4. Value Proposition
Expected benefit and existing alternatives.

## 5. Product Goals
Desired outcomes, non-goals, boundaries.

## 6. Candidate Success Metrics
Metric, baseline if known, target if validated, measurement method; mark TBD otherwise.

## 7. Constraints and Dependencies
Time, budget, technology, legal, data, people.

## 8. Assumptions and Risks
Hypotheses, validation ideas, risk severity, owner where known.

## 9. Open Questions
Unresolved decisions and next research actions.

## 10. Review and Approval
Status: Draft
Reviewer: TBD
Approval date: TBD
Outstanding conditions: TBD
```

Use links to `decision-log.md` and `assumption-log.md` when those exist. Keep the Brief concise (typically 1–3 pages for a solo project); do not force empty sections to appear complete.
