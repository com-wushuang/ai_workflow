# Requirements Review Gate

Review every criterion and report Pass / Needs Work / Unknown with evidence:
1. Approved MVP Scope exists and every requirement traces to it.
2. No scope expansion without an explicit user decision.
3. Actors, triggers, main/alternate flows and business rules are unambiguous.
4. Acceptance criteria are observable, testable and include important failures.
5. Dependencies, integration assumptions, permissions and data concerns are visible.
6. Nonfunctional needs are justified and measurable where known.
7. Contradictions, duplicates, missing decisions and unresolved risks are listed.
8. PRD and stories are internally consistent; IDs and links work.
9. User has explicitly approved the final version.

Do not auto-approve. If unresolved blockers remain, keep status Draft or Needs Revision. Approval of this gate authorizes completion of Requirements only, not UX or development.
